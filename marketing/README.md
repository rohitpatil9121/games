## Store blurb

**TIDEFALL** — *The sea goes out. The ruins come up. Get back before it returns.*

Twice an hour the sea around a forgotten island drains away, and a drowned temple town rises out of the
sand. Two to four crews of beachcombers raid it for coins, relics and idols, and race the flood back to
their camp. Everything you carry slows you down; an idol will sink you in deep water. Shove a rival from
behind to make them drop their haul, grapple onto a ruin to beat the water, or light a flare to dazzle
whoever is chasing you. Six tides, each lower than the last, the deepest wreck opening at sunset.

Up to 8 players online, bots for empty places, and a full offline Practice mode. Free, no ads, no purchases.
Runs in the browser. Keyboard and mouse, gamepad, or touch.
