Games

Browser games I made on Projection Lab, a WebGL engine I wrote from scratch. There's no three.js and no bundler, just plain ES modules. Every game below plays right here in the browser.

  • SKYFARM

    Grow a garden on an island in the sky, then glide to the next one. Storms, nights and an airship to build.

    • Farming
    • Solo
    • Co-op up to 4
    Controls

    W A S D walk or steer the glider, mouse looks, Space jumps and opens the glider, E or click uses, Q picks the next seed. Gamepad and touch work too.

    Co-op needs the game's Node server (npm start). Solo plays here.

    PlayCode

  • TIDEFALL

    The sea goes out, the ruins come up. Raid them and get back to camp before the tide returns.

    • Team raid
    • 2 to 4 teams
    • Bots
    Controls

    W A S D move, mouse looks, Space jumps, swims up or climbs. Every key can be changed in Settings.

    Practice against bots plays here. Online rooms need the game's Node server.

    PlayCode

  • BREACH

    A fast arena shooter: two to eight players, five weapons, five-minute rounds.

    • Shooter
    • Deathmatch
    • Bots
    Controls

    W A S D move, mouse aims, left click fires, 1 to 5 pick a weapon. Every key can be changed in Settings.

    Practice against bots plays here. Online rooms need the game's Node server.

    PlayCode

  • NIGHT MARKET

    A street-food tycoon where the staff and the regulars are the game. Ten nights, and rent is due every one.

    • Tycoon
    • Solo
    Controls

    Click a person or a stall to see what they're doing, click an empty spot to buy a stall. Drag or A D to move along the street, wheel to zoom.

    PlayCode

  • ORBITAL

    A gravity puzzle. Place worlds, bend the comet's path, reach the light.

    • Puzzle
    • Solo
    • Sandbox
    Controls

    Drag a planet from the dock onto the grid, drag it again to move it. Space launches the comet, hold F to fast-forward.

    PlayCode

  • REWIND HEIST

    A time-loop stealth heist. Every loop is replayed by a ghost of your past self, so put your selves to work and get the gold out.

    • Stealth
    • Puzzle
    • Solo
    Controls

    W A S D or click to move, F uses or hides, G grabs, T throws, hold Shift for slow motion. E ends the loop, R retries it, Tab plans the timeline.

    PlayCode

  • NEON RUSH

    A synthwave runner. Dodge, double-jump and chase near misses down an endless neon track.

    • Runner
    • Arcade
    • Solo
    Controls

    ← → or A D change lanes, Space, W or ↑ jumps, P pauses, M mutes.
