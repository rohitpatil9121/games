SKYFARM

Grow a garden on an island in the sky. Glide to the next one.

A new farm replaces the autosave (your three saved slots stay). Press again to start over.

Free, no ads. Keyboard and mouse, gamepad or touch.

Play together

Up to four gardeners on one farm. The host's farm is the one you all play on, and only the host's is saved.

Saved farms

The autosave is kept every time you sleep, every few minutes, and when you leave the page.
